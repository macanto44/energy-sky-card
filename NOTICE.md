## Demo only

`demo/icons.js` holds four icon paths from **Material Design Icons** (`@mdi/js`, Pictogrammers), Apache License 2.0 — https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign-JS. They are used by the local demo page and the README screenshots only, never shipped in the card's bundles. The card itself draws its icons with Home Assistant's own `<ha-icon>`.
